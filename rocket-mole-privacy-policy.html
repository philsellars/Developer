Rocket Mole

Privacy Policy

Introduction

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rocket Mole ("the App"), developed by PS Labs, handles your information.

Rocket Mole is designed to respect your privacy. The App does not collect personal data, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not use third-party information.

Information We Collect

PS Labs does not collect, transmit, or store personal information from your use of Rocket Mole.

Game Center

Rocket Mole supports Apple Game Center sign-in for gameplay features such as player identity, achievements, or leaderboards where available. Any Game Center information is provided and processed by Apple under your Apple account and Apple's own terms and privacy practices.

PS Labs does not receive or store your Apple account credentials.

Data Storage

Game progress and preferences, if stored by the App, remain on your device or within Apple's platform services associated with your Apple account. PS Labs does not operate its own servers for Rocket Mole user data.

Third-Party Services and Advertising

Rocket Mole does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or data-brokering services. Aside from Apple services required to distribute and support the App, the App does not rely on third parties to collect or process your information.

Children's Privacy

Because PS Labs does not collect personal information through the App, Rocket Mole does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
