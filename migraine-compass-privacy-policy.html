Migraine Compass

Privacy Policy

Introduction

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Migraine Compass ("the App"), developed by PS Labs, handles your information.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights in relation to it.

Health Information You Enter

Migraine Compass is designed to help you log migraine episodes, symptoms, triggers, medications, and related notes. Information you choose to enter into the App may include health-related data.

This information is stored locally on your device unless you choose to use optional Apple-managed device backup features that are outside the control of PS Labs.

Data Storage

PS Labs does not operate servers or databases for Migraine Compass. Data you enter into the App is not transmitted to or stored on PS Labs infrastructure.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or health-related data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data We Do Not Collect

PS Labs does not directly collect, transmit, or store:

  • Your migraine logs or symptom history on PS Labs servers
  • Account registration details for use of the App
  • Payment card details
  • Location data
  • Health-related information for advertising purposes

Third-Party Services

If Migraine Compass offers premium features, any billing is handled through Apple's in-app purchase system. PS Labs does not receive or store your payment details.

Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for information about Apple's handling of your data.

Children's Privacy

Migraine Compass is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
