Migraine Compass

Privacy Policy

Introduction

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Migraine Compass ("the App"), developed by PS Labs, collects, uses, and protects your information.

Migraine Compass is designed to help users track migraines and identify patterns over time while keeping data sharing to a minimum.

Information We Collect

To provide and improve the app experience, Migraine Compass may collect the following information:

  • Name: used to personalise your app experience.
  • Date of birth: used to improve age-relevant app insights and experience.
  • Gender (optional): used only if you choose to provide it to improve app experience and insights.
  • Migraine and symptom data: information you choose to log in the app, such as migraine events, symptoms, triggers, and related tracking entries.

How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve Migraine Compass.
  • To personalise your experience in the app.
  • To analyse anonymised migraine data so we can identify trends and return insights into migraine patterns.
  • To support premium features, including weather and barometric pressure information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

In premium mode, the App shares location data with Apple only as needed to retrieve weather and barometric pressure data for app features.

Database and Anonymised Data

Data collected in our database is anonymised and used for collating migraine information across users. This helps us generate broader insights into migraine patterns and improve the value of the App.

We use anonymised data for analysis and product improvement rather than to identify individual users.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple services: in premium mode, location data may be shared with Apple to retrieve weather and barometric pressure information.
  • Apple App Store: if you download the App through Apple, your use of the App Store is also subject to Apple's terms and privacy practices. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold. We retain data only for as long as needed to operate, improve, and support Migraine Compass, subject to legal and operational requirements.

Your Choices

You may choose whether to provide optional gender information. If you choose not to provide optional information, some personalisation or insights may be more limited.

Children's Privacy

Migraine Compass is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
