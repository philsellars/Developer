Migraine Compass

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Migraine Compass ("the App"), developed by PS Labs ("we", "us"), handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy. Please read this policy carefully.

The short version

  • Your data is yours — everything you log stays on your device.
  • We do not operate servers or databases for Migraine Compass; nothing you enter is sent to PS Labs.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your health data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • The App reads and writes Apple HealthKit data only with your explicit permission, and only to enhance your experience.
  • A future opt-in community-insights feature will share only anonymised, aggregated data — never anything that identifies you.
  • Questions? pslabsdev@gmail.com

1. Data you enter into the App

Migraine Compass is designed to help you log migraine episodes, symptoms, triggers, medications, notes, water intake, caffeine intake, and menstrual cycle information. All information you enter is stored locally on your device.

This data is not transmitted to or stored on PS Labs infrastructure. We have no access to it. If you use Apple's iCloud backup or device-to-device migration features, Apple's own terms and privacy policy govern that transfer — those features are outside the control of PS Labs.

2. HealthKit

With your permission, the App may read from and write to Apple HealthKit. This allows Migraine Compass to correlate health metrics (such as sleep, heart rate, and menstrual data) with your migraine patterns to provide insights.

  • HealthKit access is optional; you choose which data types to share during onboarding or in iOS Settings.
  • HealthKit data is processed on-device and is not transmitted to PS Labs.
  • We will not use HealthKit data for advertising or sell it to data brokers.
  • You can revoke HealthKit permissions at any time via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Migraine Compass.

3. WeatherKit

The App uses Apple WeatherKit to retrieve local weather conditions that may correlate with your migraine patterns. Weather lookups are made directly from your device via Apple's service. PS Labs does not receive your location or any weather request data. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for information about how Apple handles WeatherKit requests.

4. Aura Intelligence

Aura is the on-device intelligence layer in Migraine Compass. Resilience scores, insights, and correlations surfaced by Aura are computed locally from your own logs. This processing happens entirely on your device; no log data is sent to PS Labs or any third party to generate these insights.

Where the App uses Apple Intelligence features (available on supported devices), those are provided by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy.

5. Community insights (planned opt-in feature)

A future version of the App will offer an optional community-insights feature that allows you to see anonymised, aggregated patterns from other users (for example, common triggers during certain weather conditions). This feature is disclosed here in advance as referenced in the App's EULA.

  • Participation will be strictly opt-in — you must actively choose to enable it.
  • Only anonymised, aggregated data will ever leave your device; no personally identifiable information or individual log entries will be shared.
  • You will be able to withdraw at any time, and any contribution you made cannot be retroactively identified or removed from aggregated statistics.
  • This policy will be updated with full details before the feature is released.

6. In-App Purchases and subscriptions

Migraine Compass Premium is billed through Apple's in-app purchase system. PS Labs does not receive or store your payment card details. All billing, subscription management, and refund handling is carried out by Apple under their own terms and privacy policy. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

7. Data we do not collect

PS Labs does not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Your migraine logs, symptom history, or any health data on PS Labs servers
  • Account registration details (no account is required to use the App)
  • Payment card or financial details
  • Precise or approximate location data
  • Usage analytics or behavioural telemetry sent to PS Labs
  • Health-related information for advertising purposes

8. Children's privacy

Migraine Compass is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at pslabsdev@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps.

9. Your rights

Because PS Labs does not hold your personal data on its own systems, most data rights (access, rectification, erasure) are exercised directly on your device. You can delete all App data by deleting the App from your device.

If you are in the United Kingdom or European Economic Area, you have rights under UK GDPR / GDPR including the right to raise concerns with a supervisory authority. For questions about how your data is handled, contact us at pslabsdev@gmail.com.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will change, and continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Migraine Compass is a tracking aid, not a medical device. Always consult a healthcare professional about your migraines.
